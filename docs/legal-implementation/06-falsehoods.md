# 6. Falsehoods this site must never tell

**DRAFT. Last updated: TODO.** Do not substitute another unsupported claim. Every public promise needs implementation and evidence.

- Do not claim guaranteed legal compliance, a COPPA certification or attorney endorsement that has not been established. This package is an attorney-review draft.
- “We never collect child photos” is false when identifiable gallery or video media is collected.
- “There are no trackers” requires a production network/configuration audit, not just an empty source-code search.
- “Parents type the information, so COPPA always applies” is legally inaccurate; “parent-only means COPPA can never apply” is also unsafe. Actual audience and collection matter.
- “An email checkbox verifies a parent” or “the last four card digits are sufficient VPC” is unsupported. Verification method, timing and evidence matter.
- “Payment proves all permissions” is false. A purchase does not grant media, sponsor, marketing or annual safety consent.
- “All children in this video consented” needs subject-by-subject evidence, including opponents and background subjects.
- “All coaches are screened” needs cleared, unexpired records and request-time enforcement for every relevant adult.
- “Health notes are encrypted” requires actual field/object protection and restricted keys, including uploads/backups, not just HTTPS.
- “You can delete everything instantly” ignores justified legal evidence, vendor tasks and bounded backups. Explain precisely what remains and why.
- “We stop all future use when you withdraw” requires suppression at delivery/publish time, queued-job checks and cache/vendor action.
- “The waiver releases our negligence toward minors” contradicts this draft's inherent-risk-only design.
- “We have insurance for your child's medical bills” or “we have no such coverage” requires confirmed insurance facts; the input is TODO.
- “Full refund before practice” is not established by the owner's short refund note. Resolve actual terms first.
- “Your form was sent” is false when the website only opened a mailto draft. Confirm server delivery or accurately describe the mail action.
- “Your registration is complete” requires paid/free status, required consents, PDF evidence and applicable safety eligibility; a processor redirect alone is insufficient.
- “GitHub is connected to automatic deployment” is false until deployment/synchronization is configured and tested.
- “All requested protections are live” is false merely because these documents were committed. Use the implementation-status matrix and executed QA evidence.
