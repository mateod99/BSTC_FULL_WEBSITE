## Deliberate departures from unsafe or ambiguous brief details

1. Direct notice and approved verification precede child collection; a post-enrollment email is supplemental.
2. `electronic_attestation_no_payment` remains a recording label but cannot itself mark VPC verified. No child identity documents or biometrics are used.
3. Normal checkout requires a signed waiver; a proposed card-first verification pathway must be separately reviewed and cannot be a hidden bypass.
4. Notice typography uses24px rather than22px beside16px body to meet the five-point difference.
5. A plain STOP can be sender-wide even with separate message categories. No attempt to bypass carrier suppression for weather alerts.
6. Free-flow health notes become available only after verified consent, not only after payment. Gender is optional; school remains hidden.
7. Public group photos/streams require every identifiable subject, not only a single media_assets.child_id.
8. Seven-year retention is proposed for minimal evidence, subject to necessity and counsel, not asserted mandatory for every record. No invented insurance or refund entitlements.
9. No EU targeting or full GDPR package is assumed. Private-club facts do not establish a school-purpose exception or school obligations; school contracts trigger new review.
10. No final policies or new backend are represented as deployed. Existing marketing intake/media and the separate payment repository need implementation/audit before launch.
